How Aperture Works

Paste a link

Drop in any website address. Nothing is installed, and nothing needs your account.

We read what's public

Aperture reads privacy policies, cookie notices, and disclosed data practices already published by the site.

You see it plainly

Legal language becomes a clear picture of what's collected, why, and where it may travel.

Why Privacy Matters

Most consent happens without understanding.

Privacy policies are long, legal, and rarely read. Aperture doesn't judge a website — it simply makes its practices visible, so the decision to share is an informed one instead of an assumed one.

"You can't weigh a trade you can't see."

No account required

Analyze any site instantly. There's nothing to sign up for.

No personal data stored

The URLs you check are never saved, logged, or linked back to you.

About Aperture

A lens, not a ledger.

Aperture analyzes publicly available privacy information — the policies and notices a website has already chosen to publish — and presents it plainly. It holds nothing back from you, and it holds nothing of yours.
